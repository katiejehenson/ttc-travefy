1

Inbound lead

Starts when a new inquiry comes in.

  • Create the contact Manual step

    Add the new lead as a contact in Travefy.

  • Hold the consultation call Manual step

    Dana runs the consultation call. Notes from the call are saved on the contact record so everything about the client lives in one place.

2

Consultation recap

Starts when the consultation call is complete.

  • Draft the recap email Manual step

    Use whichever source fits how the notes were taken:

    • Typed notes: copy them from the contact record in Travefy.
    • Handwritten notes: take a photo and upload it to an AI tool to draft the recap.

  • Send the recap Manual step

    Paste the recap into an email and send it from Gmail or from Travefy. Travefy is connected to Dana's email, so either one works and the message is sent from her address.

3

Agency agreement and planning fee

Starts when the recap email has gone out.

  • Send the agency agreement Inactive automation

    An inactive automation in Travefy sends the form containing the agency agreement, along with a note that the planning fee invoice will come from Stripe.

  • Send the planning fee Manual step

    The fee can also be sent through Travefy: open Automations, create a new one, and select the fee option. This can also be set up inside a task list.

The fee amount is left blank on purpose. Dana enters the amount she agreed with the client on the consultation call.

Because both the agreement and the fee are sent through Travefy, the signature and payment show up there automatically.

4

Planning

Starts when Dana has received the signed agreement and the paid planning fee. She then creates the trip in Travefy and applies the planning task list.

  • Create the trip Manual step

    Set up the trip in Travefy for this client.

  • Apply the planning task list Task list

    Task lists don't apply automatically, so this is added by hand. The list is kept light and covers:

    • Proposal follow-up and updates
    • Special occasions or medical requirements
    • Visa and health notice email (see below)

  • Visa and health notice email Inactive automation

    Carried over from Tern and left inactive so Dana can decide when it's useful, for example on an FIT trip or a cruise. The client opens the Sherpa link in the email and checks the entry requirements for their destination, which puts some of that responsibility in their hands.

5

Booked

Starts when the trip is booked, meaning the client has paid a deposit.

  • Apply the booked task list Task list

    Two tasks, designed to work for any kind of trip.

  • Add trip-specific tasks Manual step

    Dana adds whatever this particular trip needs. Examples include booking flights to meet a cruise or arranging a car rental.

  • Issue final paperwork Manual step

    A reminder to send the finalized trip information about two weeks before departure, whether the trip is an all-inclusive, a cruise, or an FIT. This could become an automated walkthrough email with a link to Dana's Calendly so the client can book a time to go over everything.

6

Pre-departure and post-travel

What changed from Tern

The Tern workflows were reviewed stage by stage and rebuilt in Travefy. The main differences:

AreaIn TernIn Travefy
New vs existing clientsSeparate workflows for new and existing clients.One planning task list for everyone. Travefy can't auto-apply task lists, so splitting them adds work without a benefit.
Task listsLonger lead workflow with numbered steps.Lightweight lists focused on what actually needs a reminder.
Agreement and feeCall recap and planning invoice handled as a lead task.Agreement form sent by an inactive automation; fee sent through Travefy, with the amount entered by Dana.
Visa and health noticeSent as part of the workflow.Kept as an inactive automation, sent at Dana's discretion.
Booked tripsLong pre/post task list.Two tasks: add trip-specific tasks and issue final paperwork.
