1

Inbound lead

Starts when a new inquiry comes in.

Add: TTC Trip Leads
  • Create the contact in Travefy and add the TTC Trip Leads task list.
  • Dana holds the consultation call and saves her notes on the contact record.

Moves on when the consultation call is complete.

2

Recap, agreement and planning fee

Starts when the consultation call is complete. Work through TTC Trip Leads.

  • Send the recap email. Copy the notes from the contact record, or photograph handwritten notes and use an AI tool to draft it. Send from Gmail or Travefy; both use Dana's email.
  • Send the agency agreement form and the planning fee invoice from the list's automations.

The fee amount is blank on purpose. Dana enters the amount agreed on the call.

Moves on when the signed agreement and the paid fee both show in Travefy.

3

Planning

Starts when the agreement is signed and the planning fee is paid.

Add: TTC Planning
  • Once the proposal is sent, collect traveler details with the traveler information form and passport upload.
  • Check for special occasions or medical requirements.
  • When the client confirms, send the credit card authorization form and, if it applies, the entry and travel requirements email. The client uses the Sherpa link in it to check their destination.

Moves on when the client pays a deposit. Move the trip to Booked.

4

Booked

Starts when the deposit is paid and the trip is moved to Booked.

Add: TTC Booked - All Trips

Then add the list for the trip type:

FIT

TTC Booked - FIT

Supplier paperwork, hotel confirmations and an arrival welcome email.

Resort or all-inclusive

TTC Booked - Resort Stay

Air seats, reservation amenities and flight-to-transfer timing.

Cruise

No TTC cruise list yet. The cruise lists from Travel Biz Boss are on their own page.

  • Send the deposit paid or booking confirmed email.
  • Schedule the payment emails for each payment due date, or send them now. They are inactive until Dana does.
  • Send the travel insurance email if it applies.

From here the dated tasks and emails run on the timeline below.

What changed from Tern

The Tern workflows were reviewed stage by stage and rebuilt in Travefy.

AreaIn TernIn Travefy
New vs existing clientsSeparate workflows for new and existing clients.One set of lists for everyone. Travefy can't auto-apply task lists, so splitting them adds work without a benefit.
Before bookingLonger lead workflow with numbered steps.Two short lists, TTC Trip Leads and TTC Planning, mixing tasks with automations.
Visa and health noticeSent as part of the workflow.An automation in TTC Planning, sent at Dana's discretion.
Booked tripsLong pre and post-travel task list.One list for every trip, TTC Booked - All Trips, plus a short add-on list for FIT or resort stays.
