1

Inbound lead

Starts when a new inquiry comes in.

  • Create the contact and add the TTC Trip Leads task list Manual step

    Add the new lead as a contact in Travefy, then add the TTC Trip Leads task list so the recap, agreement and fee steps are ready once the call is done.

  • Hold the consultation call Manual step

    Dana runs the consultation call. Notes from the call are saved on the contact record so everything about the client lives in one place.

2

Recap, agreement and planning fee

Starts when the consultation call is complete. Work through the TTC Trip Leads task list added when the contact was created.

TTC Trip Leads Task list

  1. Send recap email Task

    Copy the call notes from the contact record in Travefy, or photograph handwritten notes and upload them to an AI tool to draft the recap. Paste it into an email and send from Gmail or Travefy. Travefy is connected to Dana's email, so either sends from her address.

  2. Send Agency Agreement & Planning Fee invoice Task

    The reminder to send both items below. Once the client signs and pays, both show up in Travefy.

  3. Automatic Form - Trio Travel Company Agreement Automation

    Inactive. The default trigger in Travefy is 1 day before itinerary start date. Dana either picks a fixed date to send or chooses Send Now.

    Sends the agency agreement form, with a note that the planning fee invoice will come from Stripe.

  4. Automatic Fee Automation

    Sends the planning fee invoice.

The fee amount is left blank on purpose. Dana enters the amount she agreed with the client on the consultation call.

3

Planning

Starts when Dana has received the signed agreement and the paid planning fee. Apply the TTC Planning task list to the trip. Travefy can't apply task lists automatically, so this is done by hand.

TTC Planning Task list

  1. Proposal sent - Send Traveler profile form Task

    Once the proposal has gone out, send the client the traveler profile form (the automation below).

  2. Automatic Form - TTC Traveler Information Form with Passport Upload Automation

    Collects traveler details and a passport upload.

  3. Special occassions or medical requirements? Task

    Check whether the trip involves a special occasion or any medical requirements.

  4. Client confirms trip - Send cc authorization form Task

    When the client confirms the trip, send the credit card authorization form below.

  5. Automatic Form - TTC Credit Card Authorization Form Automation

    Inactive. The default trigger in Travefy is 1 day before itinerary start date. Dana either picks a fixed date to send or chooses Send Now.

    Sends the credit card authorization form.

  6. Client confirms trip - Send entry & visa email (if applicable) Task

    Dana decides whether to send the email below, for example on an FIT trip or a cruise.

  7. Automatic Email Message - Preparing for Your Journey: Entry & Travel Requirements Automation

    The entry and travel requirements email. The client opens the Sherpa link in it to check what their destination requires, which puts some of that responsibility in their hands.

  8. Move trip to BOOKED Task

    Once the client has paid a deposit, change the trip status to Booked and move on to the next stage.

4

Booked

Starts when the trip is booked, meaning the client has paid a deposit.

  • Apply the booked task list Task list

    Two tasks, designed to work for any kind of trip.

  • Add trip-specific tasks Manual step

    Dana adds whatever this particular trip needs. Examples include booking flights to meet a cruise or arranging a car rental.

  • Issue final paperwork Manual step

    A reminder to send the finalized trip information about two weeks before departure, whether the trip is an all-inclusive, a cruise, or an FIT. This could become an automated walkthrough email with a link to Dana's Calendly so the client can book a time to go over everything.

5

Pre-departure and post-travel

What changed from Tern

The Tern workflows were reviewed stage by stage and rebuilt in Travefy. The main differences:

AreaIn TernIn Travefy
New vs existing clientsSeparate workflows for new and existing clients.One planning task list for everyone. Travefy can't auto-apply task lists, so splitting them adds work without a benefit.
Task listsLonger lead workflow with numbered steps.Two short lists before booking, TTC Trip Leads and TTC Planning, mixing tasks with automations.
Agreement and feeCall recap and planning invoice handled as a lead task.Part of the TTC Trip Leads task list: two tasks plus automations for the agreement form and the fee, with the amount entered by Dana.
Visa and health noticeSent as part of the workflow.An automation in the TTC Planning task list, sent at Dana's discretion.
Booked tripsLong pre/post task list.Two tasks: add trip-specific tasks and issue final paperwork.
