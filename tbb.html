Cruise Onboarding (Dana to review) Task list

Three welcome emails for a new cruise inquiry, each paired with a task to send it, then a hand-off to the planning workflow.

7 items: 4 tasks, 3 automations

  1. 01. Cruise inquiry received - let's set up a call: send "Cruise inquiry received - let's set up a call" Task

  2. Automatic Email Message - Cruise inquiry received - let's set up a call Automation

  3. 02. Welcome and best practices for working with me: send "Let's get started!" Task

  4. Automatic Email Message - Let's get started! Automation

  5. 03. Info received - let's get started: send "I am working on your cruise proposal" Task

  6. Automatic Email Message - I am working on your cruise proposal Automation

  7. 04. Add Cruise: Planning Workflow Task

Against the TTC process: TTC Trip Leads has no inquiry or "let's get started" email; Dana books the call and sends a recap instead. The "working on your proposal" email would sit in TTC Planning. Item 04 points to a list called Cruise: Planning Workflow, which isn't among the Travel Biz Boss lists imported into Travefy.
