TTC Booked - All Trips Task list

When to add: Added to every trip once the deposit is paid, whatever the trip type. See this step in the process

18 items: 9 tasks, 9 automations

Stays in Travefy

The 9 automations. Five of them have no date trigger (items 3, 4, 6, 7 and 18), so they go out only when someone opens them in Travefy and chooses Send Now. The other four send on their own from the trip dates.

Moves to the Trip Tracker

The 9 tasks. Travefy can't keep tasks in order, so the Trip Tracker lists them in this order with due dates worked out from the trip dates. Each tracker task names the Travefy automation to send. The plan is to remove these tasks from the Travefy list once the tracker is in use.

  1. Send email to client re: Deposit Paid or Booking Confirmed Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Not automated. Send it yourself from Gmail or Travefy.

  2. Payment schedule Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Add task in Travefy with payment schedule due dates. Pairs with items 3, 4 and 7.

  3. Automatic Form - TTC Credit Card Authorization Form Automation

    Stays in Travefy Send Now

    No date trigger, so it only goes out when you open it in Travefy and choose Send Now. Send it when a card authorization is needed.

  4. Automatic Email Message - Final payment due + protecting your trip Automation

    Stays in Travefy Send Now

    No date trigger, so it only goes out when you open it in Travefy and choose Send Now. Send it ahead of the final payment due date.

  5. Send Travel Insurance email (if applicable) Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    If insurance applies, open item 6 in Travefy and choose Send Now.

  6. Automatic Email Message - Protect your trip investment Automation

    Stays in Travefy Send Now

    No date trigger, so it only goes out when you open it in Travefy and choose Send Now.

  7. Automatic Email Message - You're paid in full! Let the countdown begin Automation

    Stays in Travefy Send Now

    No date trigger, so it only goes out when you open it in Travefy and choose Send Now. Send it once the final payment clears.

  8. Update itinerary with final documents Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 25 days before itinerary start date

    Finish before item 9 sends. Before item 9.

  9. Automatic Email Message - Your Trip Details Are Ready Automation

    Stays in Travefy

    Trigger in Travefy: 14 days before itinerary start date

    Sends on its own.

  10. Automatic Email Message - Bon Voyage! Automation

    Stays in Travefy

    Trigger in Travefy: 1 day before itinerary start date

    Sends on its own.

  11. Update Trip Status to Traveling Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 1 day before itinerary start date

    Change the status in Travefy.

  12. Automatic Form - TTC Trip Survey Automation

    Stays in Travefy

    Trigger in Travefy: 4 days after itinerary end date

    Sends on its own.

  13. Review Trip feedback Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 5 days before itinerary end date

    Reads the survey from item 12. As set, it comes due before the survey goes out; the Trip Tracker schedules it 5 days after the trip ends. After item 12.

  14. Update Trip Status to Traveled Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 1 day after itinerary end date

    Change the status in Travefy.

  15. Automatic Form - TTC Trip Request Automation

    Stays in Travefy

    Trigger in Travefy: 180 days after itinerary end date

    Sends on its own.

  16. Commission Received? Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 55 days after itinerary end date

  17. Update Trip Status to Archived Task

    Moves to Trip Tracker

    Due in Travefy: 60 days after itinerary end date

    Change the status in Travefy, then archive the trip in the Trip Tracker.

  18. Automatic Email Message - Flight Information Needed For Your Upcoming Trip Automation

    Stays in Travefy Send Now

    Optional. Send only if you need the client's flight details.
